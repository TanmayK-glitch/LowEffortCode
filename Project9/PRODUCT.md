# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, brand managers, and potential clients browsing a digital studio's portfolio. They arrive from referrals, Awwwards, or search — typically on a desktop in a studio or home office. They're evaluating craft quality within seconds: does this studio's own site feel as good as the work they'd commission?

## Product Purpose

A single-page hero landing for a fictional creative/digital studio. The page itself is the portfolio piece — cinematic, type-driven, monochrome-with-one-accent. Success: a visitor pauses mid-scroll and asks "who built this?"

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, disciplined, confrontational. The studio doesn't ask for attention; it commands it with oversized serif typography that bleeds off the viewport and a near-black atmosphere that lets every glyph land like a movie title card.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero-metric cards and gradient CTAs
- Pastel/cream/warm-neutral "AI-made" brand sites
- Overly rounded, soft, "friendly" design systems
- Cookie-cutter agency templates with stock team photos and identical card grids
- Glassmorphism-heavy dashboards masquerading as brand surfaces

## Design Principles

1. **Typography is architecture.** The letterforms are the hero — not imagery, not illustration, not icons. Every layout decision serves the type.
2. **Restraint amplifies accent.** One lime-green dash in a monochrome world hits harder than a full-palette riot. Earn every color.
3. **Cinematic pacing.** Each element enters like a scene in a film — staggered, deliberate, with weight. Never dump everything on screen at once.
4. **Bleed means confidence.** Type that overflows the viewport signals a studio that doesn't apologize for taking up space.
5. **Atmosphere over decoration.** Film grain, hairline rules, and slow-rotating sunbursts create mood without cluttering the composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: slow marquee, drop entrance animations, keep content accessible
- Visible keyboard focus states on all interactive elements
- Sufficient contrast ratios: body text ≥4.5:1, large display text ≥3:1
- Semantic HTML landmarks and heading hierarchy
